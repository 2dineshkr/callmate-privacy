Privacy Policy

CallMate is a phone, messaging, contacts and productivity application designed to help users manage calls, SMS messages, contacts, reminders, tasks and calendar-related activities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CallMate may access, why that access is required and how that information is handled.

1. Information CallMate Accesses

Depending on the features you choose to use and the permissions you grant, CallMate may access certain information stored on your Android device.

👤
Contacts

Used to display contact information, identify callers, search contacts and provide contact-related features.

☎
Call Logs

Used for recent calls, call history, summaries and call-related functionality.

💬
SMS & MMS

Used when CallMate has the appropriate/default messaging role to display, receive, send and manage messages.

📅
Calendar

Used when you choose calendar features to display relevant information or create events requested by you.

📷
Camera

May be used for supported functionality that requires camera access, such as supported video-call features.

🔔
Notifications

Used for calls, messages, reminders, tasks, events and other relevant application activity.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by CallMate is used to provide features requested by you.

  • Displaying contacts and caller information
  • Displaying and managing call history
  • Initiating phone calls
  • Displaying, receiving, sending and managing SMS messages
  • Providing reminders and notifications
  • Displaying and creating calendar events
  • Supporting CallMate's call and messaging productivity features

3. Local Data Processing

CallMate is designed primarily to operate using information available on your device.

Information such as contacts, call history, messages and calendar information is accessed as necessary to provide the corresponding application features.

✓ CallMate does not use your contacts, call logs, SMS messages or calendar information for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Sharing

CallMate does not sell your personal information.

Information may leave the application when you explicitly initiate an action requiring another application or service, such as opening a web link, making a phone call, composing an email, opening a map, or performing another user-requested external action.

5. Advertising, Analytics & Tracking

The current version of CallMate does not include advertising, third-party analytics or user-tracking SDKs.

6. Permissions

CallMate requests Android permissions only when they are required for application functionality.

Depending on the features you use, these permissions may include access to contacts, call logs, phone functionality, SMS, camera, notifications and calendar information.

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the corresponding CallMate feature from working.

7. Default Phone & SMS Roles

Some CallMate functionality requires the application to hold an appropriate Android default role, such as the default Phone or SMS application role.

CallMate requests permissions associated with these roles only when required for the corresponding functionality. Access to role-restricted information is stopped when CallMate no longer holds the required role.

8. Data Security

CallMate is designed to minimize unnecessary access to personal information and to use Android's permission and application security mechanisms to protect access to device information.

Users should also keep their Android device protected and up to date to help safeguard information stored on the device.

9. Children's Privacy

CallMate is not designed specifically for children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CallMate's functionality or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or CallMate's privacy practices, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on CallMate's Google Play Store listing.
